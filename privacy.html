Privacy policy

Last updated: 2026-10-23

What we store

The games save your progress and settings (such as sound on/off) in your own browser's local storage. This data stays on your device; we do not receive it. You can delete it at any time by clearing your browser's site data.

What we collect

We do not ask for an account, a name or an email address, and the games do not send your progress to us.

Fonts

Some games load web fonts from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address when the font is downloaded; see Google's privacy policy.

Hosting

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may log technical information such as IP addresses for security purposes; see GitHub's privacy statement.

Changes

If we add analytics or advertising in the future, this page will be updated first and will describe what is collected and how to opt out.
