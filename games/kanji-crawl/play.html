  1. A yokai appears with a kanji. Pick the right answer to attack. Answer fast for a critical hit.
  2. Wrong answers or running out of time let the enemy hit you. The correct answer is shown so you learn it.
  3. Watch the enemy's next move: 💥 Charging hits harder if you miss but staggers if you answer; 🛡️ Guarding halves damage unless you answer fast.
  4. After each fight, choose one of three relics. Each floor ends with a boss built from your weakest kanji.
  5. Your progress on every kanji is saved between runs. Floor 2 adds readings, floor 3 asks you to find the kanji from its meaning.

Keys: 1–4 to answer, H for a hint.

Full version in the works: JLPT N4 to N1 (2,000+ kanji), more yokai and relics. This free N5 edition stays free.
