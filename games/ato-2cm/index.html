🏋️ Ato 2 Senchi (Two More Centimeters)

Open in new tab

A muscle-by-muscle bench press game. Push, wobble and make the lift.

About the game

Control each side's chest and arm separately to bench press a loaded bar. The chest is strong low but pushes the bar toward your neck; the arms are strong high up. Get through the sticking point, keep the bar level, wait for the referee's calls and rack it to get three white lights. Practice, or enter a three-attempt meet.

How to play

A/S = left chest/arm, K/L = right arm/chest, Enter = rack up, Space = call a spotter. On-screen buttons on touch devices. Text is in Japanese.
