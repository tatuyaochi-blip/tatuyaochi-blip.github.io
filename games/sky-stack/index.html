🏗️ Sky Stack

Open in new tab

Tap to drop sliding blocks and build the tallest tower you can.

About the game

Sky Stack is a one-tap stacking game. A block slides back and forth above your tower; tap to drop it. Any part that hangs over the edge is sliced off, so each block can get smaller. Land a perfect drop to keep your width, and chain three perfects to grow it back. The higher you build, the faster blocks move and the sky turns from day to sunset to starry night. Try the daily tower to compare heights with friends.

How to play

Tap, click, or press Space / Enter to drop the block.
