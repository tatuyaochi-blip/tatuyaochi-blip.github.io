🍓 Tile Trio

Open in new tab

Pick tiles from the stack and match three of a kind before your tray fills.

About the game

Tile Trio is a cozy tile matching puzzle. Fruit and veggie tiles are piled in layers; tap any tile that nothing covers and it drops into your tray. Three of the same vanish. The tray only has seven spaces, so think about which tile to take next. Every level is built so it can be cleared. When you get stuck you get one Undo, one Shuffle and one Send 3 Back per level. A daily stack gives everyone the same puzzle.

How to play

Tap or click a free tile to move it to the tray. Keyboard: Tab to a tile and press Enter. U undo, B send back, S shuffle.
