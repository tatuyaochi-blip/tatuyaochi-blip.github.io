🌸 Block Bloom

Open in new tab

Fit blocks on an 8×8 garden and fill rows to make flowers bloom.

About the game

Block Bloom is a calm block puzzle. You get three pieces at a time; drag them onto the 8×8 garden. Fill a whole row or column and it bursts into petals and clears. Clear lines on back-to-back moves to build a combo, wipe the whole board for a big Perfect bonus, and keep going until no piece fits. There is no timer, so think as long as you like. A daily garden gives everyone the same pieces.

How to play

Drag a piece onto the board, or tap a piece and then tap a square. Keyboard: 1–3 pick a piece, arrow keys move, Enter places.
