🔋 Denchi Hitotsu (One Battery)

Open in new tab

A railgun distance game: one battery of power, split between launch and flight.

About the game

You have exactly 100 units of power. Hold to charge the railgun and release to fire, then tap during flight to spend what is left: hop over ponds, bounce off the ground or open the glider's wings. Pick a rolling, bouncing or gliding shot, smash through crates and fences, and beat the rival's flag across three courses: plains, ice field and desert.

How to play

Hold to charge and release to fire. Tap during flight to use the remaining power. Text is in Japanese.
