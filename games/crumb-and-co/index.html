About the game

Crumb & Co. is a cozy idle bakery. Knead dough to earn your first coins, hire apprentices, build ovens and grow into a pastry empire. Every two minutes a new daily special sells three times as fast, rush orders pop up for quick bonuses, and your bakers keep working while you are away. When you are ready, open a new branch to earn stars that boost everything forever.

How to play

Tap or click the bread to knead. Tap a row to buy; switch between ×1, ×10 and Max. Tap rush orders before they leave.
