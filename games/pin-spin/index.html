🎯 Pin Spin

Open in new tab

Throw pins into a spinning log without hitting the ones already stuck.

About the game

Pin Spin is a quick one-tap timing game. A log spins at the top of the screen; tap to throw a pin into it. Land every pin to clear the stage, but if your pin hits one that is already stuck, it is game over. Logs speed up, slow down and even change direction, and every fifth stage is a boss log with extra pins. Hit the apples on the rim for bonus points, and try the daily challenge where everyone gets the same logs.

How to play

Tap, click, or press Space to throw a pin.
