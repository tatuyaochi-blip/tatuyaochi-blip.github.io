Lucky Pips

Roll five dice. Build combos. Collect charms that bend the rules. Beat nine rounds and three bosses.

  1. Each round has a target score. You get a few hands to reach it.
  2. Tap dice to hold them, then reroll the rest (a few rerolls per hand).
  3. Score the hand: (chips + pips of the scoring dice) × mult. Bigger combos give more.
  4. Between rounds, spend coins on charms. Unused hands and saved coins pay extra.
  5. Every third round is a boss with a nasty rule.
