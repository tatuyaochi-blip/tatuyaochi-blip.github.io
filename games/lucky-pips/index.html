🎲 Lucky Pips

Open in new tab

Roll five dice, build combos, collect charms and beat three bosses.

About the game

Lucky Pips is a quick dice roguelite. Each round has a target score and a few hands to reach it. Hold the dice you like, reroll the rest, and score combos from a simple Pair up to Five of a Kind. Your score is chips × mult: between rounds, spend coins on charms that bend the rules, and find a build strong enough for all nine rounds.

How to play

Tap or click dice to hold them, then Reroll and Score hand. Keyboard: 1–5 hold, R reroll, Enter score.
